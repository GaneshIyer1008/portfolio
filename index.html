Who I Am

I'm a Master's student in Robotics, Systems & Control at ETH Zürich (2025-2027), with a passion for Reinforcement Learning, Robot Learning, and Legged Robots. My research focuses on combining classical control theory with modern deep learning to create robots that can learn and adapt in dynamic environments.

Previously, I graduated with honors in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Bombay (Rank 2/196), where I developed a strong foundation in dynamics, control systems, and robotics.

Core Interests: Legged locomotion, sim-to-real transfer, learning-based control, motion planning, and the intersection of classical and learning-based robotics.
